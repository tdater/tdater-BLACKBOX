The problem SCATTERED CONTEXT

A typical technical project spans tools that don't talk to each other. That works until the project gets complicated, gets interrupted, or grows several overlapping failures.

Where the facts live

  • Screenshots show what happened.
  • Logs hold the diagnostic details.
  • Notes explain what was changed.
  • AI conversations hold possible explanations.
  • Source control records the code changes.
  • Memory is expected to connect everything else.

Questions that get hard

  • Which build introduced this behavior?
  • What exactly changed before the problem appeared?
  • Did that adjustment help, or did the test conditions change?
  • Have I already tried this?
  • What was the last known-good configuration?
  • What should I do next?
  • How do I brief a coding agent without rebuilding the whole context?

BLACKBOX makes those answers part of the project record instead of something you reconstruct over and over.

The core idea A CONNECTED RECORD

A project is more than a conversation or a folder of attachments. BLACKBOX keeps its goals, questions, evidence, environment and configuration, changes, tests, outcomes, remaining unknowns and next actions, and the links between them.

The result is a history that explains what happened and why the next step makes sense.

Core workflow SIX STEPS

Saving an observation never waits on an AI response, an internet connection or a long form. Capture first, organize later.

Create an investigationStart with one problem: "Live OBD values stop updating after the head-unit screen turns off." Record what should happen, what does, and the starting conditions.
Capture evidenceText, photos, screenshots, logs, files, voice notes and measurements, saved immediately.
Record the environmentDevice, OS, build, connected hardware, connection mode and settings. Old tests keep their original snapshot.
Define a testThe question, prerequisites, steps, expected behavior, the one variable changed and what stays constant.
Record the resultPass, fail, partial, inconclusive or not finished, with evidence. A pass proves that run's expectation, not that the project is fixed.
Compare and continuePick the next test, keep a known-good reference, add a regression check, hand off, or pause with a clear resume point.

Core features SINCE 0.1.0

Projects and investigations

One project holds several investigations, each with its own evidence, tests, status and next action, so the broad project and the specific problem stay distinct.

Evidence timeline

A chronological record of observations, attachments, changes and results, linked to the investigation or test that gives them meaning. Originals and later interpretations stay distinguishable.

Structured test runs

Reusable plans with revisions. Each run is recorded against a specific build and environment snapshot, with expected and actual behavior kept separate.

Resume capsules

Objective, last known-good state, current failure, last tested change, next action and relevant evidence on one screen. Marked STALE once newer activity makes it outdated.

"What changed?" comparisons

Compares recorded information between two runs: known changes, known matches, missing or unknown values, different outcomes, and conditions that make the comparison unreliable. Missing information is never shown as proof that nothing changed.

You review it before copying, saving or sharing.

Project archives

Export and import a whole project with its attachments and relationships, checksummed. Importing always creates a new project, so nothing gets overwritten.

Regression memory

Confirmed past failures become checks to revisit before you accept a new build. Project-specific, built from your actual history.

New in 0.2.0 FOR MORE THAN BUGS

Start from a template

Car, home, network, software, making, garden, health, kitchen or custom. Each picks the details it tracks and suggests readings. Everyday words by default (Problem, Try, Findings), with a Technical switch for the old vocabulary.

Two-tap capture

A home-screen widget and a quick-settings tile for a note, photo, voice note, barcode scan or reading. Anything without a home lands in an Inbox.

Readings with charts

Log voltages, temperatures, weights or speeds and see each one charted over time with range and trend.

Reminders and checklists

"Check this again in three days" reminders, plus checklists saved from tests that worked. Each use is recorded as its own run, step by step.

Seen this before?

Problems and notes surface similar records from your other projects, matched on shared words like error codes and part names.

Photos that read

Pull the text out of a photo or decode a barcode or QR code, on the phone. Dictation uses the phone's offline speech model only.

PDF reports and backups

One tap turns a problem into a PDF with photos. Pick a backup folder (SD card, USB stick, or one your sync app watches) and BLACKBOX keeps an archive per project there.

Locked down

The records database is encrypted with a key held by Android Keystore. Optional fingerprint or screen-lock gate, and a switch that hides BLACKBOX from the recent-apps screen.

Four looks

Flight recorder, Daylight, Midnight, or colours taken from your wallpaper.

Fold-native ONE DEVICE, THREE WORKSPACES

The Fold is treated as a workspace that changes shape, not a phone layout stretched across a bigger screen. It stays useful on ordinary phones and other Android form factors.

Compact

Quick evidence capture, project selection, resume summaries and focused single tasks.

Expanded

Related information side by side: investigation and evidence, expected and actual, two runs, timeline and next action.

Tabletop

Reference material on the upper half; capture controls, test steps and result entry on the lower half.

Continuity: folding, unfolding, rotating or resizing never loses a draft, duplicates a test, restarts a capture or resets the active investigation. This is tested on emulators; behavior on the Fold8 itself is still being verified.

Evidence before answers FIVE KINDS OF KNOWLEDGE

CategoryMeaning
OBSERVEDRecorded by you, imported from an artifact, or directly measured.
INFERREDA possible explanation or interpretation.
TESTEDAn action performed under recorded conditions.
SUPPORTED RESULTA specific outcome established by that test.
UNKNOWNMissing, conflicting or not yet established.
  • An AI suggestion never silently becomes a confirmed fact.
  • A correlation is never presented as proof of causation.
  • Conflicting observations stay visible instead of being overwritten into a cleaner story.
  • "Unknown" is a valid and useful result.

Optional AI assistance OFF BY DEFAULT

AI speeds up the investigation without replacing its evidence. The project record stays fully useful with AI unavailable, disabled or never set up.

What it helps with

  • Summarizing selected records.
  • Spotting missing information.
  • Suggesting hypotheses.
  • Proposing a test that separates competing explanations.

In 0.1.0 this is one well-integrated provider: a LiteRT-LM model file you import, running on the phone. No key, no account, no bill.

Rules it follows

  • You start every request.
  • It only sees records you select, from projects that allow it.
  • Its output stays separate from original evidence and is saved only as an inferred claim.
  • Records it cites must exist, or the answer is discarded.
  • Errors are reported honestly. No hidden cloud fallback.
  • It never runs commands it proposes.

Privacy and local-first NO ACCOUNT · NO CLOUD

Local-only projects

Excluded from every AI request. The rule lives in the app's request-handling logic, not just the interface. BLACKBOX doesn't even request internet permission, and every release is checked for that automatically.

Deliberate sharing

You review what goes into a handoff or export. Private attachments, sensitive logs and credentials are never included automatically; a scanner flags likely secrets before you share.

No silent monitoring

BLACKBOX doesn't watch your device or collect activity from other apps. Any future capture feature will use supported access methods and explicit action.

Honest boundaries

Local-only controls what the app uploads. It isn't a claim of complete device security or forensic protection. Handle work information according to your organization's policies.

Example investigation INCLUDED AS THE SAMPLE PROJECT

Problem

OBD readings stop updating after the screen turns off. The adapter stays connected, RPM freezes, a log of the session is imported, and the build and adapter details are recorded.

Test

Repeat the screen-off sequence while changing one configuration value. The outcome is attached to that run with its actual environment and evidence.

Comparison

BLACKBOX shows the recorded difference and the different outcomes. It does not declare that the changed setting caused the improvement.

Handoff

A fix request with the reproduction steps, tested changes, results and selected logs. The coding agent gets a focused brief instead of a vague description and a pile of screenshots.

What BLACKBOX is not

  • Another generic chatbot, or an always-recording surveillance tool.
  • A universal controller for every installed app.
  • A replacement for an IDE or source control.
  • A guarantee of diagnostic accuracy, or a substitute for proper physical measurements and safety procedures.
  • A pile of disconnected features behind a dashboard.

Its value is keeping the problem, its evidence, the tests performed and the next useful action connected.

Install 0.2.0 · ACTIVE DEVELOPMENT

  1. On the phone, open the latest release and download the APK under Assets.
  2. Open the file. When Android asks, allow your browser or file manager to install unknown apps.
  3. Tap Install, then pick a template or load the labeled sample project to look around. Updating from 0.1.0 is designed to keep your records and encrypts them on first launch; export an archive first if they matter.
